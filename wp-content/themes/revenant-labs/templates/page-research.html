RESEARCH USE

How materials are supplied, how documentation is matched to a lot, and the conditions under which Revenant Labs supplies research organisations in Australia.

SUPPLY CONDITIONS

Intended use

Materials are supplied for legitimate in-vitro laboratory research only. They are not for human or veterinary use, and no use guidance is provided with them.

Documentation

Where analytical documentation has been issued for a lot, it is published against that lot. Reports are shown as issued, without restatement or summary.

Eligibility

Purchasers must be 18 or over and buying for legitimate laboratory research. A research-use declaration is required at checkout on every order.
