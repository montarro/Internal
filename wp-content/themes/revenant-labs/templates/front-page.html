FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Are Revenant Labs products intended for human use?

No. All materials supplied by Revenant Labs are for legitimate in-vitro laboratory research only. They are not for human or veterinary use, and no guidance on human or animal use is provided.

Where can I find the COA?

Where analytical documentation has been issued for a lot, it is published in the Lab Reports library and shown on the relevant product page. If no report is listed for a lot, none has been published for it.

Is the packaging discreet?

Orders are dispatched in plain, unbranded outer packaging. Product identification and any accompanying documentation remain inside the shipment.

How quickly are orders dispatched?

Dispatch times are listed on the Shipping & Returns page. Tracking details are issued once an order has been dispatched.

How should unopened research materials be stored?

Storage conditions for a given material are listed in the storage section of its product page where that information has been provided. No handling, preparation or administration guidance is supplied.

Who is eligible to purchase?

Orders are accepted only from purchasers aged 18 or over who are buying for legitimate laboratory research purposes. A research-use declaration is required at checkout.
