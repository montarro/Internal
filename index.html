FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

How much does landscaping cost?

Every yard is different, so we price each job after seeing it. Smaller jobs like a new lawn start from a few hundred dollars, while full makeovers with paving and walls run into the thousands. Your free on-site quote is a clear fixed price with no obligation.

How long will my job take?

A turf lay is usually one to two days. Paving and retaining walls typically take a few days to a week depending on size and access. We give you a realistic timeframe in writing before we start.

How do I book?

Call 0457 357 085 or send the quote form below. We'll confirm a time to visit, measure up and quote — usually within a few days.

Which suburbs do you service?

We work right across Melbourne's north-west, including Craigieburn, Mickleham, Sunbury, Point Cook, Melton and Werribee. Not sure if you're in range? Give us a call and we'll let you know.

What are your hours?

We're on the tools Monday to Friday 7am–5pm and Saturday 8am–2pm. You can call during those hours, or leave a message any time and we'll get back to you.
