FAQ

Common questions

Are these products for human use?

No. All materials are supplied strictly for in-vitro laboratory research and development. They are not for human or veterinary use, and no medical, diagnostic or therapeutic claims are made.

Do I get a certificate of analysis?

Yes. Each production batch is third-party tested by HPLC and LC-MS, and the certificate is matched to the lot number printed on your vial.

Why don't all listings show a purity percentage?

A purity figure only appears once it has been verified for the exact batch you'd receive. If a number isn't shown yet, the batch COA is the source of truth.

How is my order shipped?

Orders are dispatched cold-chain from Western Australia on express tracked services, in discreet outer packaging, Australia-wide.

How should materials be stored?

Keep refrigerated on arrival. Every vial is labelled with a "keep refrigerated" instruction and its lot number for your records.

What is bacteriostatic water for?

It's a laboratory reconstitution solvent listed for research handling of lyophilised reference materials. Like everything here, it is research-use only.
