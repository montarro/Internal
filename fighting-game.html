How to play

Pick a Meep for each player, then choose 1P vs AI or 2 Players. First to win 2 rounds wins. Getting hit costs health but never stops you. Jump to flip, and you can kick in mid-air. Your gold meter fills over time — when it's full, hit special to unleash your Meep's power.

Player 1

  • A / D — move left / right
  • W — jump (flip!)
  • F — punch
  • G — kick (works mid-air)
  • S — block (hold)
  • R — power (when meter full)

Player 2 (the AI in 1P mode)

  • ← / → — move left / right
  • ↑ — jump (flip!)
  • K — punch
  • L — kick (works mid-air)
  • ↓ — block (hold)
  • / — power (when meter full)
