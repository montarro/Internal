Kombat Arena

An original 2-player fighting game prototype. First to win 2 rounds takes the match. Land hits, block incoming strikes, and back off to recover.

Player 1 AD move · W jump
F punch · G kick · S block
Player 2 / AI ←→ move · ↑ jump

Tip: blocking cuts damage but you can't move. Time your strikes.
