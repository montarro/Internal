How to play

Pick a pup for each player on the start screen, then choose 1P vs AI or 2 Players. First to win 2 rounds takes the match. Getting hit costs health but never stops you — you can keep moving and swinging.

Player 1

  • A / D — move left / right
  • W — jump
  • F — punch
  • G — kick
  • S — block (hold)

Player 2 (the AI in 1P mode)

  • ← / → — move left / right
  • ↑ — jump
  • K — punch
  • L — kick
  • ↓ — block (hold)
